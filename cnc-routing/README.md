Includes future CNC machining **and** purpose-built router sleds/jigs made using existing woodworking tools.

## Active
- [NUT65 miniature precision router sled](projects/nut65-router-sled.md)
- [Future full custom walnut GH60 case](projects/walnut-gh60-case.md)
- [Desktop CNC research and purchase gate](research/desktop-cnc.md)

## Process priorities
1. Measure actual part and understand wall thickness, clamping and datum surfaces.
2. Prototype fixture and geometry in wood/plastic.
3. For metal: qualify the **specific router/tool/carriage** on scrap of appropriate alloy before working on a finished component.
4. Document cut quality, accuracy, runout/deflection and part retention rather than relying on motor wattage.
5. Avoid machining unsupported thin bezel surfaces or assuming shallow passes alone remove grabbing/chatter risk.

The proposed Bosch-router metal sled is **experimental**, not a proven or certified milling configuration.
