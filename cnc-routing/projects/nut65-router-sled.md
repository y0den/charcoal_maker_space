# Charcoal Mini Router Sled — NUT65 bezel

**Status:** Concept; no jig built or aluminum milling verified.

## Objective
Reusable miniature precision two-axis router sled with a replaceable fixture. First use: controlled straight-edged decorative pocket in the removable **black NUT65 aluminum upper bezel**. Also useful for wood routing, Clock OS hardwood pockets and small joinery.

## Existing tools
- Bosch 1617-style 2.25 HP corded router, with fixed and plunge bases; separate router table.
- Ryobi P601 compact fixed-base trim router.
- X1C for custom cradles, stops and test inserts; laser for templates/registration.

## Functional concept
- Stiff base and support beneath the entire workpiece.
- Removable **bezel cradle** indexed from physically measured edges/features, with no pressure on fragile unsupported spans.
- Two supported axes or a constrained single-axis channel plus indexed shifts; hard stops must limit cutter travel.
- Router base supported outside the narrow bezel; controlled and measurable Z referencing; chip/visibility management.
- Modular carriage plate so a woodworking trim-router mode can be separate from any qualified metal configuration.

## Required measurements
- Exact bare bezel geometry, underside pockets/ribs, flat contact area and local wall thickness.
- Desired accent width, length, corner geometry and setback from chamfers.
- Available travel, router collet-to-carriage distance, fixture stack height, clamps.

## Gates before real bezel
- [ ] Paper/CAD alignment verified against physical bezel
- [ ] Cradle successfully holds mockup without flex or shifting
- [ ] Walnut/wood pocket made to target dimensions
- [ ] Aluminum-appropriate bit, machine speed range, fixture safety and operating method confirmed for this specific router
- [ ] Scrap 6063 aluminum trial demonstrates acceptable pocket edge, repeatable depth and no grabbing/chatter
- [ ] Bezel local remaining metal thickness independently checked
- [ ] Final toolpath clearances and removal/assembly verified

**Do not prescribe feed/speed or full 1/8-in aluminum recess until part and cutter are characterized.** A 1/8-in walnut starting blank can be thinned underneath to meet the bezel's safe pocket depth.
