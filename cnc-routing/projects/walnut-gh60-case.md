# Future custom walnut GH60 case

**Status:** Future project, not designed or machined.

Goal: machine a purpose-designed solid walnut case for a GH60-compatible PCB, learning from the MK61 mounting experiments.

Requirements:
- Accommodate complete ~60% keyboard case blank and workholding; actual case envelope is a **design input**, not a fixed nominal dimension.
- Adequate Z clearance and cutter reach for cavity depth and outside profile.
- Deliberately engineered mounting supports, gasket or tray configuration, USB-C port and stabilizer/plate clearances.
- Optional separately machined copper/brass bottom weight if desired.
- Compare geometry/sound changes against the existing walnut MK61 rather than assuming a heavier case alone solves the sound.

Cross-reference: [MK61 acoustic build](../../projects/mk61-walnut.md).
