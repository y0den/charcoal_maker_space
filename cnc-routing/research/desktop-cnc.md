# Desktop CNC purchase research — hold / evidence needed

**Status:** No desktop CNC bought. Current plan prioritizes the existing-router sled experiment.

## User requirements
- A future **large-format (~4-foot)** woodworking CNC is the preferred machine for cabinetry and furniture.
- A compact machine is only attractive if it brings **genuinely useful precision metalworking**, principally aluminum bezel milling and a possible copper weight.
- Needs enough true usable workholding/cutter travel for a whole 65% bezel and custom 60% walnut case.
- Accuracy, stiffness, support and active owner community matter more than fast material removal.
- User explored $400–$500, then ~$1,000 and $1,200–$1,400 ranges; none authorized/purchased.

## Shortlist examined (prices are historical discussion figures, NOT live quotations)
- Genmitsu 3020-PRO Ultra: stronger metal-oriented compact option, but ~300 × 205 mm travel creates a width constraint for complete 65% bezel.
- Genmitsu 4040-PRO and VEVOR S4040: 400 × 400 mm, but lightweight motion systems/smaller spindles are not obvious precision-metal winners.
- LUNYEE 4040 EVO / Nova: larger bed; more complete linear rail/ball screw hardware on Nova, but price/availability/community evidence unresolved.
- AnoleX 4030-Evo Ultra 2: 400 × 300 mm class, promising metalworking mechanics and published aluminum demos, but limited long-term owner history compared with mature brands.
- FoxAlien Vasto and Shapeoko: potentially stronger shared community/project documentation; evaluate specific version and availability rather than brand alone.

## Must-pass purchasing checklist
- [ ] Confirm current price and actual included spindle/controller/accessories
- [ ] Measure full usable tool travel **and** clamp/carriage clearance for bezel
- [ ] Independent **owner-funded** aluminum pocket and finish examples, not only promotional engraving
- [ ] Evidence of brass/copper profiling if copper weight is a priority
- [ ] Documented backlash, deflection and reliability/repair issues
- [ ] Replacement parts, manuals, support responsiveness, active owners/community
- [ ] Final delivered cost including proper cutters, stock, fixture and measuring gear

No machine should be called a precision metal mill from spindle wattage or nominal linear rail specifications alone.
