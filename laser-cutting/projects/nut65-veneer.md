# NUT65 walnut veneer first-pass

**Status:** Black NUT65 and Real Wood Paper walnut sheets received (2026-11-27); no template, laser test or physical fit result documented yet.

## Desired result
A precision-fit genuine walnut accent on the stock wide upper black aluminum bezel. Mode Encore is an aesthetic reference only; preserve visible black reveal and factory case opening. This first pass is reversible or low-risk if possible.

## Candidate material
Real Wood Paper walnut paper-backed veneer, previously discussed as 0.016-inch (~0.406 mm) in 11 × 17-inch long-grain format. Confirm the **received sheets' actual thickness and backing** before final design. Product reference:
https://realwoodpaper.com/product/walnut-paper-back-016-11x17-long-grain/

## Build plan
1. Measure physically received NUT65 upper bezel; don't draw from promotional images.
2. Create CAD/SVG outer perimeter, keywell cutout and optional separate rear accent; reserve black anodized reveal.
3. Cut paper/cardstock to check every inner corner and top-edge clearance.
4. Test laser power/speed/kerf on small pieces of the actual sheet, considering backing and charred edges.
5. Cut real walnut with the desired grain across the board, test fit dry, then choose compatible thin adhesive and finish by sample.
6. Document photo, grain orientation, dimensions, file version, and any lifting/surface issues.

## Go/no-go for a recess
If the flat veneer treatment already satisfies the look, preserve the bezel. Otherwise refer to [router sled](../../cnc-routing/projects/nut65-router-sled.md) for a **separately qualified** recessed solid walnut approach.
