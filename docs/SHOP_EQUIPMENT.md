# Workshop equipment and constraints

## Available tools (user-confirmed)
- **3D printer:** Bambu Lab X1 Carbon, AMS, 0.4 mm nozzle. eSUN PLA+ for fit tests; Bambu PLA Matte Black preferred final appearance when suitable.
- **Laser:** 20W diode; wood and suitable acrylic; **does not machine aluminum or POM**.
- **Routers:** Bosch 1617-style corded combo with both fixed and plunge bases plus router table; Ryobi P601 ONE+ cordless trim router.
- **Wood shop:** hardwood scraps and normal woodworking, sanding/finishing and jig-making tools.

## Not yet acquired
- Desktop CNC for precision aluminum/brass/copper work
- Future large-area CNC with roughly 4-foot-capable travel for woodworking and cabinets

## Practical process selection
- Laser: thin walnut veneer, 2D decorative or registration templates.
- X1C: fixture prototypes, hidden mounting parts, mockups and test-fit components.
- Hand routing / custom sled: wood pockets, constrained channels; aluminum *only after separate tool, fixture and scrap validation*.
- Metal-oriented CNC: candidate if precision aluminum pockets and copper weights cannot be accomplished suitably with current tools.
