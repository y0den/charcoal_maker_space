# Walnut MK61 / GH60 acoustic build

**Status:** Existing custom walnut keyboard; mount/support hypothesis under test.

## Fixed preferences
- Keep the richly grained walnut case.
- Keep GMK Stealth Colorized keycaps.
- Target is deep, full, solid sound comparable to the user's tray-mounted GK61; not simply maximum softness.
- All other mounts, plate, dampening and PCB details can be revised if supported by tests.

## Known history
- The earlier 1.2 mm flex-cut GH60 PCB and soft/unsupported mounting arrangement did not deliver the desired sound.
- Adding temporary center supports improved results; support geometry is leading hypothesis, not a proven sole cause.
- Experimenting with printed support posts, Poron contact surfaces and tray/burger mount. New non-flex GH60 PCB has been ordered; verify installed state before documenting a final configuration.
- Screw-in PCB stabilizers are present. Oil Kings and POM/FR4 plate choices are under investigation.

## O-ring trial
- White silicone rings already found in shop: **4 mm OD × 1 mm ID × 1.5 mm section**.
- Red silicone rings ordered: **4 mm OD × 2 mm ID × 1 mm section, 50 Shore A**.
- Potential arrangement: one ring below the PCB, or a ring above and below each tray screw; track compression and screw engagement.
- Post plus compressed ring stack height must be checked against existing case ledge and USB-C position. USB-C opening adjustment is a later fit operation, not a reason to accept bad mounting geometry.

## Next tests
1. Record precise post heights, mounting hole positions and PCB thickness.
2. Test printed Poron-topped supports before adding new variables.
3. Compare O-ring stack and screw tension using the same switches/keycaps and a consistent sound test.
4. Log improvements and failures rather than assuming gasket or burger mounting inherently makes deeper sound.
