# Clock OS and Govee physical hardware

**Status:** Enclosure design work, not a released manufactured design.

## Context
Charcoal Clock OS uses a 3.9-inch ESP32 display; Unit 2 is the primary reliable platform, with a gift unit and a legacy unit also in the wider project. This repository owns **enclosure and fabrication work**, not the Clock OS firmware source.

## Desired construction
- Slim, minimal, satin/matte black printed body with a real walnut accent.
- Right-angle USB-C routing/clearance where needed.
- Display opening with precise bezel and recess geometry.
- Accessible controls, usable viewing angle, clean desk presentation.
- An optional QR code for the device's settings interface and a personalized gift finish belong to the enclosure design brief, not necessarily the final production file.

## Sensor housings
- Matching covers for two Govee H5075 temperature/humidity sensors in the gift concept.
- Do not block ventilation, sensor readings, buttons or battery access.
- A cover for the larger air-quality sensor is separate scope and needs measured dimensions.

Clearances verified with actual device, removable components fit without force, temperature/humidity reading not impaired by housing design, USB-C remains serviceable, material finish meets target.
