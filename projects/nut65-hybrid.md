# Charcoal NUT65 / 001

**Status:** Black NUT65 and Real Wood Paper walnut sheets physically received (user confirmed 2026-11-27); inspection, measurement and baseline testing not yet documented.
**Goal:** One-of-one black aluminum NUT65 with real walnut, preserving stock mechanical quality, mounting, and desired deep acoustic character.

## Non-negotiables
- Do not transplant the internals into an untested wood housing.
- Preserve PCB/plate/gasket architecture, quick-open function, connectors and controls.
- Modifications are cosmetic to the **existing metal case** until measured tests justify otherwise.
- Do not claim an acoustical improvement from a cosmetic weight/inlay without recordings or A/B results.

## Design route
1. **Baseline:** Assemble/use the NUT65 as purchased; document PCB, plate, gaskets, switches, sound and photos.
2. **Low-risk first pass:** Laser-cut Real Wood Paper walnut veneer for a top/rear accent; intentional narrow black anodized reveal. Check opening clearance, corner fit, adhesive compatibility and finish on scrap.
3. **Optional solid-wood inlay:** ~1/8-in (3.175 mm) walnut desired visually. Measure the removable bezel **top and underside** before choosing any recess depth. Wood thickness may be reduced on underside to preserve metal integrity.
4. **Possible future detail:** Custom removable copper weight, matching existing fastener positions and hardware accessibility only after measured factory-part inspection.

## Geometry and constraints — pending measurement
- Full bezel outer length, width, thickness, flatness and top chamfer
- Rear bezel usable accent length and width
- Distance to keycaps/switch plate and case opening seam
- Underside ribs, catch/clip areas and minimum remaining wall after a recess
- Existing bottom weight attachment, dimensions, connector and dongle clearances

**Do not use nominal keyboard dimensions as machining coordinates.**

## Acceptance criteria
- Walnut reads as intentional, cleanly integrated material (no lifting edges or irregular reveal).
- Keyboard remains serviceable and unchanged mechanically.
- No structural thinning below measured safe limit.
- Inlay flushness and pocket geometry measured, not judged only by photo.
- Before/after typing sound recorded with unchanged acoustic setup if assessing sound.

- Prototype paper/cardstock, then veneer on the laser.
- Decide whether the appearance already meets the target before undertaking aluminum cuts.
